Cord Board is a place where Discord users can show up, connect, and naturally meet new people.

You log in once with Discord OAuth, and your profile is automatically created and added to a live board that updates as more people join.

Instead of focusing on servers, Cord Board puts people first.

Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Supabase, Discord OAuth, HTML, JavaScript, JSON
